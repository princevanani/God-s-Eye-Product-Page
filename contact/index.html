Before you write in

A few things that help us help you faster:

  • Which site the issue happened on, and what broke specifically
  • Your Chrome version, found at chrome://version
  • Whether toggling the site off in the popup fixes it — that tells us whether it's a fingerprint-masking issue or a blocking-rule issue
